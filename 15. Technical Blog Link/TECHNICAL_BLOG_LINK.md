### Article Summary & Key Takeaways
1. **The Modern Warranty Problem:** How manual verification causes delayed customer payouts and multi-million dollar fraud losses across consumer brands.
2. **Dual-Model Decision Architecture:** Why combining tabular feature models (XGBoost) with visual summary card classifiers (Teachable Machine/MobileNetV2) catches subtle document contradictions.
3. **Decoupling Business Policies:** How decoupled JSON rule engines allow business managers to adapt warranty terms without requiring code deployments.
4. **Transparent Explainability in AI:** Moving away from black-box decisions by providing natural-language rationales and mandatory reviewer override logs.
